Refunds & Returns

6 Questions
You can initiate a return request within 7 days of delivery by visiting Customer Support and providing your Order ID and photo of the sealed package.
Unopened healthcare devices, non-prescription supplements with intact tamper-evident seals, and personal care products are eligible for return. Opened pharmaceutical medications cannot be returned due to strict clinical hygiene regulations.
Approved refunds for returned items are disbursed within 3-5 business days via direct bank transfer or UPI payout.
If an item is damaged during transit, notify us within 24 hours. We arrange an immediate free replacement dispatch with express delivery.
Our quality assurance team cross-checks all orders. In the rare event of a mix-up, we collect the incorrect item and deliver the correct product on the same day at no extra cost.
For Cash on Delivery orders, our claims team will request your preferred bank account or UPI ID to credit the refund directly.

Our registered clinical pharmacists check the doctor's registration number, patient name, prescribed dosage, and validity date before packing your order.
