Login & Account Management

How do I verify my phone number or email?

When creating your account or editing your profile, your phone number and email are securely verified with single-sign-on validation. You don't need to re-verify every time you login.

Can I reactivate an inactive account?

Yes, you can simply sign in with your registered email or phone number. If you need assistance, click "Forgot Password" or message support.

How do I update my delivery addresses?

Navigate to Edit Profile to add or modify multiple saved addresses.

Delivery & Packaging

How are temperature-sensitive medicines shipped?

All temperature-regulated products (e.g. insulin, specialized serums) are packed in insulated thermal pouches with cooling gel packs to maintain optimal temperature throughout transit.

What is the estimated delivery timeframe?

Standard orders are delivered in 24 to 48 hours in metro areas, and 2-4 business days nationwide.
