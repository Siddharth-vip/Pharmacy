Medicine Awareness & Safe Consumption

Healthcare medications and botanical supplements work best when used responsibly. Here are key clinical guidelines every patient should understand:

1
Medicine Categories & Purpose

Understand the difference between daily preventative vitamins (OTC) and targeted therapeutic prescription medicines.

2
Common Precautions & Dosages

Always review dosing instructions on packaging. Do not double up doses if a routine supplement was skipped.

3
When Professional Advice is Essential

If you are pregnant, nursing, taking chronic heart medication, or experiencing unexpected symptoms, consult your physician immediately.
